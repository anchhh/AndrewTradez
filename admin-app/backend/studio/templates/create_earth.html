Step 2: Capture the view

Screenshot Earth with the extension, then bring it back here with the Create tab. Take as many views as the flight needs — a front angle, a top-down, the run itself. They appear below; one of them is the one the path gets drawn on, and you choose which.

{# Several views of one property: a front angle, a top-down, the line the flight takes. One of them is drawn on, and which one is a choice -- marked rather than assumed, because the path lives in that picture's coordinates and cannot move to another. #}
